⏱️ 1. 50Hz Temporal Cycle Segmentation

Door motor current telemetry is a continuous 50Hz stream. The segmentation algorithm detects cycle boundary transients:

Cycle Onset: I(t) > 0.50A sustained for ≥ 60ms (3 samples)
Cycle Termination: I(t) < 0.20A and |dI/dt| < 0.05 A/s
Direction Detection: Sign of motor driving voltage / H-bridge polarity

⚡ 2. Mechanical Friction & Obstacle Feature Space

DC motor torque is directly proportional to armature current ($\tau = K_t \cdot I$). When foreign objects or guide rail friction impede the door, motor current surges:

• Peak Motor Current (I_max): Normal < 4.0A | Abnormal > 6.5A
• Integral Energy: E = V · ∫ I(t) dt (Joules)
• Total Travel Duration: Normal ~2.8s | Drag > 4.0s
