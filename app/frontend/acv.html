🌡️ 1. Refrigerant Subcooling Deficit Signature

When refrigerant mass leaks from a car's HVAC circuit, the condenser cannot sustain adequate liquid subcooling margin:

ΔT_sub = T_condenser_sat(P_high) - T_liquid_line
• Nominal Charge: ΔT_sub ∈ [5.0°C, 8.0°C]
• Refrigerant Leak: ΔT_sub < 1.5°C (Flash Gas at expansion valve)
• Consequence: Suction pressure P_low collapses, cooling capacity drops
