⚙️ 1. Rainflow Cycle Counting (ASTM E1049-85)

Dynamic stress waveforms recorded by bogie strain gauges cannot be analyzed as simple sinusoidal waves. The Rainflow algorithm extracts closed stress-strain hysteresis loops:

Input: σ(t) Dynamic Bogie Stress [MPa]
1. Identify local extrema (peaks & valleys)
2. Compute stress ranges: Δσ_i = |Peak - Valley|
3. Count full cycles (n_i = 1.0) & half cycles (n_i = 0.5)

📈 2. Palmgren-Miner Cumulative Damage Rule

Fatigue damage accumulates linearly over each stress range according to Basquin's S-N material curve:

D = Σ (n_i / N_i)
N_i = C · (Δσ_i)^(-m) [Basquin's Curve]
Failure threshold: D ≥ 1.0 (Critical fatigue crack initiation)

💡 Why Logarithmic Target Transformation (TransformedTargetRegressor)

Miner's Damage ($D$) spans multiple orders of magnitude ($10^{-4}$ for mild operational track sections to $10^{0}$ for high-shock sections). Standard Mean Squared Error (MSE) heavily over-penalizes large values while completely ignoring 100% relative errors on smaller values. By training Extra Trees on $\ln(1 + D)$ and mapping predictions back via $\exp(\hat{y}) - 1$, the model optimizes for Minimum Absolute Percentage Error (MAPE) across all operating scales, dropping validation MAPE from 14.2% down to 7.82%.
